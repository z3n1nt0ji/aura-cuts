Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Summary

Aura Cuts uses only what it needs to post the clips you approve. It does not sell your data, show ads, or share your information with anyone except the services listed below.

2. What we collect

  • Account info from connected platforms: for TikTok, your basic profile (open ID, display name and avatar) and the access tokens that let Aura Cuts post on your behalf. Similar login tokens for YouTube, Instagram and Twitch.
  • Your videos: recordings and clips from your own livestreams, plus the transcripts, titles and captions created for them.
  • Posting records: which clips were posted, where and when, and basic public view counts.

We do not collect your passwords, payment details, contacts or private messages.

3. How we use it

Only to create clips, show them to you for review, and publish the ones you approve to the accounts you connected.

4. Where it is stored

Videos, transcripts and tokens are stored on the creator's own computer and drive. A clip may be placed briefly in cloud storage (Cloudflare R2) so Instagram can fetch it, then deleted.

5. Services we share data with

  • The platforms you connect (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Twitch), to publish your approved clips and read your stream data.
  • An AI service (Anthropic) that receives clip transcripts to suggest highlights, titles and captions.
  • Cloudflare R2, for temporary video hosting as described above.

6. Keeping and deleting data

You can disconnect Aura Cuts at any time in each platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). Disconnecting stops all access. Local files can be deleted at any time, and tokens are removed when an account is disconnected.

7. Children

Aura Cuts is not intended for anyone under 18.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
