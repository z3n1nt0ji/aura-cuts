Aura Cuts

Aura Cuts helps streamers turn their own livestreams into short-form videos. It finds highlight moments, crops them to vertical format and adds captions. The creator reviews each clip, edits the title and caption, and chooses what to post to their own accounts. Nothing is posted without the creator's approval.

Aura Cuts is a personal tool run by its creator for their own channels.
